My story

I learn by taking things apart — then rebuilding them to find out why.

I'd read a deep-learning paper, not quite believe it, and rebuild the model myself just to see if it was true. I wondered how the web actually moves data — so I wrote an HTTP server from scratch instead of reaching for a library.

That same itch is why I write. I've published 15+ technical articles breaking down ideas like PCA and embeddings — because the moment I can explain something simply is the moment I know I finally understand it.

I won't pretend I have my whole life figured out. But I know the direction: build things that are useful, honest, and kind — and that leave the world a little better than I found it.
